About YLU

Empowering university students to learn, grow, lead, and serve.

Our Identity

Who We Are

YLU (Younglife University) is a youth-focused initiative under Young Life Cambodia (YLC), dedicated to empowering university students in Cambodia.

YLU provides opportunities for students to strengthen their learning abilities, develop leadership skills, improve their language and digital skills, build strong character, and explore the Gospel in a supportive community.

Our Journey

Our Story

Founding Date
20 July 2024
Established

YLU was established on 20 July 2024 with a vision to create a meaningful space where university students can learn, grow, connect, and develop both their skills and character.

Since its beginning, YLU has been working to support students through education, leadership development, practical skills, and faith-based activities.

Core Conviction

What We Believe

We believe university life is not only about academic achievement. It is also an opportunity to develop skills, character, leadership, and faith that can shape students’ lives and their contribution to society.
